Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What information do I need for a freight quote?

For an accurate quote, please provide: cargo type, weight, dimensions, pickup location, destination, and any special requirements. The more details you provide, the more accurate our quote will be.

How quickly can you respond to inquiries?

We guarantee a response within 2 hours during business hours (8 AM - 6 PM, Monday-Friday). For urgent matters, our 24/7 emergency line is always available.

Do you handle international freight?

We specialize in land freight transportation within Egypt, serving all major ports including Alexandria, Damietta, and Sokhna. For international shipping, we partner with reliable global logistics providers.

What are your payment terms?

We offer flexible payment terms including advance payment, credit terms for established clients, and online payment options. Specific terms are detailed in your service agreement.
