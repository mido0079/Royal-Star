Our Story

Pioneering Excellence in Egyptian Land Freight Transportation

Founded in 2018, Royal Star has emerged as a premier land freight transportation company in Egypt, revolutionizing how cargo moves across the nation. Our journey began with a simple vision: to provide reliable, efficient, and secure logistics solutions that empower businesses to thrive in the competitive Egyptian market.

As a proud member of Orient Group, we leverage the collective strength of five specialized companies, creating a diversified network that delivers end-to-end transport solutions with unmatched efficiency and precision. This strategic partnership has enabled us to build a robust infrastructure capable of handling the most complex logistics challenges.

Over the past six years, we have consistently demonstrated our commitment to excellence, safety, and customer satisfaction. Our growth trajectory reflects our dedication to innovation and our ability to adapt to the evolving needs of the Egyptian logistics landscape.

Our Journey

Milestones in Egyptian Logistics Excellence

2018

Company Foundation

Royal Star began operations with a focused vision on container transport services in Alexandria. Established with a fleet of 10 modern trucks and a commitment to excellence.

  • Initial fleet of 10 trucks
  • Focus on Alexandria port operations
  • First 50+ successful trips
2020

Service Expansion

Expanded our service portfolio to include heavy cargo transport and specialized equipment handling. Grew our fleet to 25 vehicles and expanded operational capacity.

  • Added heavy cargo division
  • Fleet expansion to 25 vehicles
  • 500+ annual trips milestone
2022

Network Growth

Strategic expansion to Damietta and Sokhna ports, establishing a comprehensive nationwide presence. Implemented advanced tracking and logistics management systems.

  • Multi-port operations established
  • Advanced GPS tracking implemented
  • 2,000+ annual trips achieved
2024

Market Leadership

Recognized as one of Egypt's top land freight providers with 4,260+ annual trips. Enhanced service offerings and strengthened partnerships across the logistics ecosystem.

  • 4,260+ annual trips milestone
  • Industry recognition achieved
  • Enhanced customer service protocols
